Does Juno count calories?

No. A photo or a note is a full meal log.

What happens if I miss a week in Juno?

Nothing breaks. XP only goes up, and today's plan is waiting.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Juno runs in your phone's browser. Add it to your home screen to open it full screen.

Is Juno free?

Yes. Juno is free to use.

Where does Juno keep my logs?

In your Juno account. Sign in with your email and they follow you to any phone.

Can I change my workouts in the app?

Yes. Swap a move, or change its sets and reps, from the Train tab.
