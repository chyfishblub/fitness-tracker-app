Pocket Spotter · five style directions · from docs/references/character-styles

Same palette, five different creatures.

Every direction uses only the six Elemental solids, Dusk, Ink and Cloud, and sits on a veil surface in the app. Each stage shows the builder-size body and the 44 px perch head.

A · Grain BlobLineage · ref 01
  • BUILD silhouette (blob, arch, burst, squircle) × eye size × grain density. No limbs to pick.
  • DEMO squash, stretch and tilt only; form cues do the teaching.
  • FIT the stipple echoes the veil blur; any solid reads on any gradient.
  • RISK reads as a sticker, not a trainer; weakest for the 51 demo loops.
B · Gym ObjectLineage · ref 02 Fukuda
  • BUILD object (kettlebell, bottle, foam roller, plate, yoga block) × face × leg style.
  • DEMO legs walk and hop; no arms, so upper-body demos pair it with a drawn bar or cable.
  • FIT two-tone body plus Dusk with a mid-century cut-paper feel.
  • RISK can read as a shop mascot; the object choice carries a lot of meaning.
C · Soft Geo BuddyLineage · ref 03 + current Juno
  • BUILD primitive (arch, squircle, cloud, quarter-circle) × face × held prop.
  • DEMO stubby arms hold a dumbbell or band; good for presses and raises, poor for hinges and legs.
  • FIT closest to the boards already on this canvas; props carry equipment meaning.
  • RISK the generic "app mascot" look; needs the grain or a prop to feel specific.
D · Bean & SticksChosen · + tankLineage · ref 04
  • BUILD bean proportion (tall, round, squat) × face × accessory (headband, cap, bands).
  • DEMO full articulation. Four line limbs, two keyframes each: the 51 loops get cheap.
  • FIT one Elemental solid plus Ink lines at the icon set's stroke weight.
  • RISK the Dumb Ways to Die association; keep limbs heavier and bodies calmer.
E · Gradient SpiritLineage · ref 01 + the app's own light
  • BUILD silhouette × eye shape × which window it glows with (auto by time of day).
  • DEMO no limbs; borrows D's stick limbs only inside the demo sheet.
  • FIT the coach is made of dawn, day and dusk: the strongest brand tie on the table.
  • RISK vanishes on its own gradient without a veil; reduced-motion must freeze the shift.
