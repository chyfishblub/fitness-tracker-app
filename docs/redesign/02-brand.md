# Juno — Step 2: Brand narrative and positioning

**Status:** draft for review · **Date:** 2026-11-13 · **Owner:** Fish
**Builds on:** `docs/redesign/01-user-problem.md` (approved 2026-11-13)

---

## 1. The story in one paragraph

Most people don't quit training because they stop caring. They quit because one busy week
makes them feel behind, and feeling behind makes them stay away. Juno is built for that
moment. It is a training journal and a small companion that treat every bit of effort as
real: one meal photo counts, a short workout counts, coming back after two weeks counts.
Nothing is a debt and nothing breaks. Over weeks, those small days add up to numbers that go
up, and Juno is there to show you the proof.

---

## 2. Positioning

**For** people who train, or want to, and keep losing the habit a few weeks in,
**Juno is** a training and food journal with a small companion that keeps you showing up.
**Unlike** calorie counters that demand precision, and streak apps that punish a missed day,
**Juno** makes every small log count and picks up wherever you left off,
**so** consistency quietly turns into progress you can see.

### Core promise

**Small days count.**

This answers the open question from step 1. Juno's engine is consistency and its reward is
progress. The promise leads with consistency, because that is where people fall off.

### Tagline candidates

1. **Small days count.** Recommended. It doubles as the promise.
2. Keep showing up.
3. Pick up where you left off.

---

## 3. Brand pillars

| Pillar | What it means | How it shows up in the product |
|---|---|---|
| **Small days count** | A partial day is a good day. The minimum is tiny. | One-tap meal log, "a photo is a complete log", short-workout option, weekly counts instead of daily perfection. |
| **No debt** | Missed days never pile up or turn red. | No breaking streaks, no "you're behind", the plan moves forward on its own, a welcome-back instead of a warning. |
| **Proof, not pressure** | Show real, honest numbers and let them motivate. | Last time versus this time on every set, weekly wins, strength trends. No scores, no grades, no food judgment. |
| **Your history is safe** | Past effort is never lost. | Visible sync state, easy guest-to-account upgrade, every goal and log kept, the next goal built on real numbers. |

---

## 4. Personality

Juno is the gym friend who has been there. Warm, plain, a bit playful, never preachy. It
notices effort, keeps things light after a slip, and gets out of the way while you train.

| Juno is | Juno is not |
|---|---|
| Warm | Gushing or cute for its own sake |
| Plain and specific | Vague, motivational-poster |
| Playful in small doses | Jokey during a hard set |
| Honest about numbers | A judge of food or bodies |
| Calm after a slip | Disappointed, guilt-tripping |

## 5. Voice and tone

### Voice rules

- **Short.** One idea per sentence. Most messages are one or two sentences.
- **Specific.** Name the real thing: "Lunch logged", "45 kg × 9", "3 of 3 workouts".
- **Effort first.** Notice what was done before mentioning what's next.
- **No debt words.** Never "behind", "missed", "failed", "broke", "catch up".
- **No food morality.** Never "cheat", "guilty", "bad", "clean", "earn it", "burn it off".
- **Facts and feelings stay separate.** A number is stated plainly; encouragement comes in
  its own sentence.

### Words we use and avoid

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| logged, showed up, back, next, this week | streak, missed, behind, failed, lost |
| heavy, satisfied, still hungry, energized | guilty, cheat, bad, junk |
| lighter day, short session | skipped, slacked |
| your history, kept, safe | reset, wiped, start over |

### Tone across moments

The three coach tones from the Pocket Spotter work stay. Each user picks one, and every
template is written in all three, falling back to Steady.

| Moment | Steady (default) | Spark | Straight-up |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meal logged | Lunch logged. Two of three today. | Lunch is in! Two of three. | Lunch logged. 2 of 3. |
| Personal best | 47.5 kg on bench. That's a new top set. | New bench best, 47.5 kg! | Bench PB: 47.5 kg. |
| Back after a gap | Good to see you. Today's plan is ready when you are. | You're back! Today's plan is waiting. | Welcome back. Today: upper body. |
| Heavy meal | Logged. Dinner's at six if you want to keep going. | Got it! Dinner's at six. | Logged. Next: dinner at 6. |
| Goal ends | Phuket's here. You trained 31 times since June. | You made it! 31 sessions since June! | Goal done. 31 sessions. Next goal? |

## 6. Naming

### The app: Juno

Fish chose **Juno** as the new app name on 2026-11-13. It works well for the brand: short,
warm, easy to say, and it sounds like a companion's name, not a tool's.

**Risk to resolve before launch.** "Juno" is crowded in health apps. A quick search found
several, including a habit and workout tracker from a developer called Juno Health, an AI
health coach called MyJuno, and Juno apps for journaling, chronic illness and fertility.
Several unrelated companies also hold "JUNO" trademarks in health-adjacent classes. Before
going public:

- Use a descriptor in store listings, such as "Juno: Training Journal".
- Run a proper trademark search in the classes that cover software and health services.
- Check domain and social handle availability.

For the redesign, Juno stays the working name.

### The coach

**Recommendation:** the coach is a **Spotter**, and each person names their own. The app is
Juno. Your spotter might be called Mo, Bean or Captain. The default suggestion in the builder
is blank, with a few starter names offered. This avoids "Juno" meaning two things, and the
naming moment makes the coach feel like yours.

In copy: "Juno" is the product ("Juno keeps your history safe"). The spotter speaks in
first person and signs with its own name.

## 7. Visual direction: pick one

Two directions are on the redesign canvas, each built from this narrative. Both use the same
Today card, coach and copy so they can be compared fairly. **Pick one, or name a hybrid.**

### A · Paper & Ink (doodle-led)

From reference `05-doodle-raincoat-umbrella.jpg`. Warm paper, wobbly ink outlines, flat fills
in cobalt, coral and mustard. The spotter is a hand-drawn blob in a tank top.

- **Fits:** "small days count" and "no debt". It feels like a journal you keep, not a
  dashboard that grades you. It is very distinct from typical fitness apps.
- **Risks:** hand-drawn assets are slower to produce, especially the 51 exercise demos.
  Wobble has to stay controlled so data and set inputs stay crisp.

### B · Daylight (Elemental-led)

From the Elemental Gradient system. Time-of-day gradients, frosted cards, Archivo and Source
Serif. The spotter is the Bean & Sticks figure in a Dusk tank.

- **Fits:** "proof, not pressure". It is calm, premium and quiet.
- **Risks:** gradients and glass are common in wellness apps, so the brand is less
  distinct. It is cooler and less personal than the narrative.

**Recommendation: A, Paper & Ink.** It expresses the narrative best and gives Juno a look no
competitor has. To manage its risks, keep the hand-drawn wobble to characters, icons and
decorative frames. Data, numbers and inputs stay in clean type.

## 8. What this decides for the design system (step 3)

- Copy rules and the banned-word list feed every component's microcopy.
- No component may show a red failure state for a missed day or a meal.
- The spotter is named by the user; the builder needs a name field with starter names.
- The chosen visual direction defines color, type, line and texture tokens.
