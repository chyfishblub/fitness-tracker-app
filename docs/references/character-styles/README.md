# Character style references — Pocket Spotter

Saved 2026-11-10 and 2026-11-13 from the user. Reference only: none of these may be copied; they set the
direction for the coach's silhouette, face, texture and limb language. The app's colors come
from the Elemental Gradient palette (see `docs/REFERENCE.md`), never from these images.

| File | What it is | What to take from it |
|---|---|---|
| `01-grain-blobs.webp` | Stock set of abstract blob/arch/burst shapes, flat fill with a stippled darker grain at the base, large white-ringed eyes, tiny mouths, strong emotions. | Silhouette variety, the grain-at-the-base shading, eyes as the whole personality. |
| `02-toru-fukuda-drawing-wonder.png` | Toru Fukuda, *Drawing Wonder 0459* (2012). Everyday objects (cup, tub, chair, fish, plant, TV) given a face and two little flat legs. Mid-century two-tone print feel. | Object-as-character, cut-paper flatness, the walking legs, two-tone restraint. |
| `03-shape-characters-with-props.png` | "Shape character" vector set: soft geometric primitives (star, arch, heart, square) with stubby arms holding brushes, cups and easels. | Props in hand, stubby arms and feet, a cloud-colored face plate, friendly group composition. |
| `04-dumb-ways-to-die-beans.png` | *Dumb Ways to Die* character poster (Metro Trains Melbourne / McCann). Bean bodies with thin stick limbs, dot eyes, small accessories. | Thin articulated limbs that animate cheaply, bean body as the universal base, accessories that read at tiny sizes. |
| `05-doodle-raincoat-umbrella.jpg` | **Preferred direction (2026-11-13).** Hand-drawn doodle trio on cream watercolor paper: a hooded figure in a blue raincoat, a white blob holding a coral umbrella, and a mustard umbrella with eyes. Thick, slightly wobbly ink outline, flat fills, two dot eyes, almost no mouth. | The whole feel: ink-line wobble, paper texture, flat warm-muted fills (cobalt, coral, mustard, off-white), tiny dot eyes, characters defined by what they wear or hold. |

Five style directions derived from these are on the mockup canvas
(<https://claude.ai/artifact/XJVhHrwo1xNM91XRTffSrY>, board `Styles.dc.html`) and in
`docs/mockups/pocket-spotter/Styles.dc.html`.
